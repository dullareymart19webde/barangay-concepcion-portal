Official Documents Available

Available Certificates & Requirements

Barangay Concepcion issues the following official certifications online. Please ensure you prepare the required supporting details.

Document 1

Barangay Clearance

Official certification of good moral standing and residency within Barangay Concepcion, with no pending criminal or civil derogatory record.

Common Purposes:
  • Local / Overseas Employment
  • Police Clearance / NBI Renewal
  • Business Permit / Bank Requirement
Requirements:
  • 1 Valid Government / Student ID
  • Community Tax Certificate (Cedula)
  • Minimum 6 Months Residency
Apply for Clearance
Document 2

Certificate of Residency

Attests to the applicant's bona fide residence in their respective Purok within Barangay Concepcion, Valencia City.

Common Purposes:
  • Scholarship / University Enrollment
  • Bank Account / E-Wallet Opening
  • Proof of Address for Utilities
Requirements:
  • 1 Valid ID showing Concepcion Address
  • Stated Purok & Years of Residency
  • Cedula (Optional but recommended)
Apply for Residency
Document 3 • Free

Certificate of Indigency

Certifies indigent or low-income status of a resident household for social welfare, medical, and legal assistance programs.

Common Purposes:
  • Hospital / Medical Assistance (BPH, etc.)
  • DSWD AICS & City Social Welfare Aid
  • Public Attorney's Office (PAO) Legal Aid
Requirements:
  • Valid ID of Applicant / Representative
  • Medical bill / Prescription / Endorsement
  • 100% FREE OF CHARGE by law
Apply for Indigency
